06 / FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Everything you need to know before making FlowPilot part of your routine.

What is FlowPilot AI?

FlowPilot is an AI-powered execution workspace. It turns goals into plans, adapts priorities as conditions change, protects focus time, and automates repeatable workflow steps.

Is FlowPilot another task manager?

It can hold tasks, but the core product is decision support: what matters next, when to do it, and what should happen automatically around it.

Do I need to connect all my tools?

No. The landing-page demo is self-contained, and the product is designed to deliver value before you connect deeper integrations.

Does the AI make decisions for me?

FlowPilot proposes plans and priorities. You stay in control of the goal, constraints, and final actions.

Can I use it for studying?

Yes. The planning model works well for academic deadlines, revision cycles, project work, and balancing several fixed commitments.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes core planning, focus sessions, and a lightweight activity history. No credit card is required.

How does team billing work?

Team is priced per active member. Use the calculator above to estimate a monthly total from 1–50 seats.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are presented as month-to-month or yearly billing, and you can cancel before the next renewal.
